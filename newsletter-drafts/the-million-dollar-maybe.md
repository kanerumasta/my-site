**Subject:** The Million-Dollar Maybe

**Alternative subject:** When a POC becomes a promise

**Preview text:** A major opportunity arrives before the product is ready. What should the company promise?

I've been thinking about the moment a working demo becomes a promise.

A demo proves that an idea can work. It does not prove that a company can deliver it on a deadline, support it, or recover when it fails.

[![A gold opportunity token transitioning into an unfinished blue technical blueprint and casting a question-mark shadow](https://www.macibale.site/images/the-million-dollar-maybe-editorial-hero.png)](https://www.macibale.site/blog/the-million-dollar-maybe?utm_source=buttondown&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=million_dollar_maybe)

So what happens when the opportunity is enormous, but the evidence is still incomplete?

I just published a short article about that uncomfortable space between **yes** and **not yet**.
