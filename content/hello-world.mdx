Hi, my name is Mac. I’m a software engineer who also loves playing the guitar. This blog is where I document what I learn while building projects and exploring new technologies.

## Why I Started This Blog

While working on different projects, I realized that I often forget things I have already learned. Sometimes I solve a problem and, a few weeks later, have to figure it out again.

So I decided to start writing. This blog will help me:

* remember what I’ve learned
* improve how I explain technical ideas
* track my growth as a developer

## What I’ll Be Sharing

Most posts will be practical:

* bugs I encountered and how I fixed them
* small discoveries while coding
* lessons from real projects
* things I wish I knew earlier

Nothing too fancy—just useful experiences from real work.

## My Approach

I won’t wait until I’m an “expert” to write. I’ll document what I’m learning, what confuses me, and how I solve problems, because that is often where the value is.

<Note>
**A note on scope:** I’ll favor practical lessons, implementation tradeoffs, and honest retrospectives over abstract tutorials.
</Note>

## Final Thoughts

I’m building, learning, and documenting—one step at a time. If you are building similar systems, I hope these notes save you a few hours and help you avoid a few dead ends.
